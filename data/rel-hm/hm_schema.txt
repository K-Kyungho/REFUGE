Database Description: The H&M relational database hosts extensive customer and product data for online shopping experiences across its extensive network of brands and stores. This database includes detailed customer purchase histories and a rich set of metadata, encompassing everything from basic demographic information to extensive details about each product available.

[Table]
Columns: Column type
- Pkey
- Fkey

1. article: Stores detailed information about products.
- Columns:
  - article_id: numerical
  - product_code: numerical
  - prod_name: text
  - product_type_no: numerical
  - product_type_name: categorical
  - product_group_name: categorical
  - graphical_appearance_no: categorical
  - graphical_appearance_name: categorical
  - colour_group_code: categorical
  - colour_group_name: categorical
  - perceived_colour_value_id: categorical
  - perceived_colour_value_name: categorical
  - perceived_colour_master_id: numerical
  - perceived_colour_master_name: categorical
  - department_no: numerical
  - department_name: categorical
  - index_code: categorical
  - index_name: categorical
  - index_group_no: categorical
  - index_group_name: categorical
  - section_no: numerical
  - section_name: text
  - garment_group_no: categorical
  - garment_group_name: categorical
  - detail_desc: text
- Primary Key: article_id
- Foreign Keys: None

2. customer: Stores customer profile information.
- Columns:
  - customer_id: text
  - FN: categorical
  - Active: categorical
  - club_member_status: categorical
  - fashion_news_frequency: categorical
  - age: numerical
  - postal_code: categorical
- Primary Key: customer_id
- Foreign Keys: None

3. transactions: Records purchase transactions by customers.
- Columns:
  - t_dat: timestamp
  - price: numerical
  - sales_channel_id: categorical
  - customer_id: numerical
  - article_id: numerical
- Primary Key: None
- Foreign Keys:
  - customer_id → customer.customer_id
  - article_id → article.article_id
